About IAResearchAggregator

Project Overview

IAResearchAggregator is a comprehensive tool for tracking, analyzing, and visualizing research trends in artificial intelligence. It automatically aggregates papers from arXiv, identifies emerging research topics, and provides insights into the evolving landscape of AI research.

Key Features

Fetch papers from arXiv using optimized queries. Filter by category, date range, and keywords to find the most relevant research.

  • Support for complex queries
  • Efficient batch processing
  • Automatic metadata extraction
  • Error handling and rate limiting

Advanced natural language processing to identify, validate, and categorize research topics.

  • ML-powered topic categorization
  • Quality validation
  • Optimized query building
  • Fallback mechanisms for robustness

Identify emerging research trends by analyzing recent publications and comparing with historical data.

  • N-gram extraction and frequency analysis
  • Time-based comparison
  • Growth rate calculation
  • Optional citation analysis

Rich visualizations to make trend data more accessible and insightful.

  • Interactive trend charts
  • Topic word clouds
  • Distribution analyses
  • Publication trends over time
